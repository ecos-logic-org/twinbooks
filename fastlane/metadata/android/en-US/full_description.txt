TwinBooks is a dual-pane EPUB reader designed for language learners. Open the same book in two different languages and read them side by side on your Android tablet.

Features:

- Split-screen view with left and right book panels
- Paragraph synchronization between books
- Sentence-level navigation and highlighting
- Dark theme for comfortable reading
- Table of contents navigation
- Font size adjustment
- Reading position persistence - resume exactly where you left off
- Landscape-optimized for tablets

Perfect for bilingual reading, parallel texts, and language immersion. Open an English book on the left and your native language on the right to learn vocabulary and grammar in context.
